Nails, pedicures, lashes, and skin.

At Beautique Bar, you’ll experience expertly crafted services catered to your unique style and preferences — a rejuvenating visit that leaves you feeling pampered, confident, and beautiful.

Nails

Unleash your creativity with sculpted, custom acrylic sets that are as durable as they are beautiful. Bio Gel is a fusion of beauty and health — natural-looking gel that’s nourishing for your nails. Shellac brings a flawless, mirror-like shine that lasts for weeks.

Pedicures

Luxurious spa manicures and pedicures that leave your hands and feet pampered, polished and perfect.

Lashes

Luxurious lashes, meticulously applied to enhance your natural beauty and add everyday glamour.

Skin

Nourishing facials that cleanse, hydrate and rejuvenate your skin for a healthy, radiant glow. Facial and body hair removal are tailored to your skin for a smooth, confident finish.
